Annotation Specification

  1. Listen first. Play the whole package before typing. Use 0.75x speed for fast speech.
  2. One segment = one speaker turn. If a second person speaks, cut the audio there (S at the playhead, or drag across the waveform) and give each segment its role.
  3. Write exactly what is said in Khmer script — no summaries, no grammar corrections. Keep repeated words and false starts.
  4. Numbers: write them as spoken in the annotation text; the normalized text turns Khmer digits into 0-9 automatically.
  5. English words spoken inside Khmer stay in Latin letters, and set Code Switching Present to Yes.
  6. Unclear speech: if a word cannot be understood after three listens, write [unclear].
  7. Noise, music only, or no speech: skip the segment ({{ icon("trash") }}) or the whole package.
  8. Normalized text is made automatically (word-segmented, digits 0-9); edit it only if the segmentation is wrong.
  9. Role Information describes the speaker of each role (age group, gender, native speaker); Overall Audio Information describes the whole package.
